# NULL NHL V3.8.5 — Roster & Historical Schedule Verification

- Fetches independent NHL season roster for each slate team and compares player IDs.
- Separately counts completed regular-season team games before the selected date and prior head-to-head meetings.
- Reports missing/malformed API responses as unavailable, never as zero.
- Exports per-player roster checks and per-team schedule coverage.
- Season rosters may contain historical team membership and are **not** a point-in-time transaction ledger; mismatches require manual investigation.
- Does not change rankings or claim goal-probability improvement.
